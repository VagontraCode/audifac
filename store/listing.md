# Google Play listing

Paste these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing (English) and add a French translation (Store listing → Manage translations → French).

## English (en-US)

**App name** (30 max): `audifac – Hi-Res Music Player`

**Short description** (80 max):
`Every format, FLAC & DSD, audiophile EQ, lyrics, stats. Your music, your phone.`

**Full description** (4000 max):

```
audifac plays the music stored on your phone — all of it, beautifully.

EVERY FORMAT
• FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, AC-3, DTS, TrueHD…
• DSD (DSF and DFF, DSD64 to DSD256), converted at 176.4 kHz
• Finds audio anywhere on the phone and SD card — not just the Music folder
• Labels every song with its quality: Hi-Res, Lossless, Lossy, codec and bit depth

AUDIOPHILE SOUND
• Parametric and 10-band graphic equalizer, 64-bit processing, bit-transparent when off
• Separate correction for each pair of headphones (imports AutoEQ), applied automatically
• Presets per song, album, artist, genre or source
• Volume matching (ReplayGain / EBU R128), gapless playback, optional crossfade
• Spectrum analyzer, level meters and the full signal path, from file to speaker

MADE FOR LISTENING
• Synced lyrics (embedded or .lrc)
• Smart playlists that update themselves
• Listening stats: top songs, artists, albums, hours listened
• Audiobooks & podcasts: speed control, resume where you left, chapters, skip buttons
• Sleep timer with a gentle fade-out
• Home-screen widget, Android Auto, Chromecast and DLNA casting
• Last.fm scrobbling (with your own API key)
• Backup and restore of everything in one file
• English and French

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library never leaves your phone.
```

**Category**: Music & Audio · **Tags**: Music player, Audio, Equalizer
