audifac — Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

audifac is a music player for audio files stored on your device. It is made by Georges (gtongfa@gmail.com).

What audifac collects

Nothing is collected by the developer. audifac has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

To work, audifac reads and stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • your audio files' tags (title, artist, album…), technical details (codec, sample rate) and loudness measurements;
  • your favorites, playlists, smart playlists, listening history and play counts;
  • your equalizer presets, rules and settings;
  • lyrics files you attach to songs.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The "Back up to a file" feature writes a copy to a file you choose; audifac never uploads it.

Optional features that use the network

These are off until you turn them on, and only talk to the service you chose:

  • Last.fm scrobbling. If you connect your own Last.fm account, audifac sends the title, artist, album and time of the songs you listen to, to Last.fm (last.fm/legal/privacy). Your Last.fm password is used once to log in and is not stored; only the session key is kept on your device.
  • Casting to a TV or speaker. When you choose a Chromecast or DLNA device on your Wi-Fi, audifac shares the song being played with that device over your local network. Nothing leaves your network.

Permissions

  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / storage on older Android): to find and play your audio files.
  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback): to keep playing with the screen off and show the player controls.
  • Network and Wi-Fi multicast: only for Last.fm and casting, as described above.

Children

audifac is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
