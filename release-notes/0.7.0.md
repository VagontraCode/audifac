## What's new in audifac 0.7.0

### Listen closer
- **A–B repeat**: tap the new **A–B** chip in the player to mark the start, tap again to mark the end, and that part plays on a loop, even with the screen off. The section shows on the seek bar; a third tap stops it. Made for learning a song, a solo, a language lesson.
- **Blind test (ABX)**: Equalizer → *Blind test: can you really hear it?* A is the original, B is your sound, X is one of them at random. Ten rounds tell you if you really hear your EQ or if it's luck. Levels are matched, so the louder one doesn't give the answer away.
- **Where quality is lost**: the Signal sheet (tap the quality label in the player) now starts with a verdict: *Nothing lost on the way*, or exactly where quality changes: a lossy file, Android resampling, 16-bit output, the limiter, Bluetooth. Bluetooth gets its own step, because it always re-encodes the music.

### Lyrics, audiobooks and podcasts
- **Lyrics from the internet**: no lyrics in the file? Lyrics → **Search online** finds them on LRCLIB, synced line by line when available, and keeps them on your phone. Untidy titles are understood too ("Never Enough WMA", "Joeboy - Show Me (Official Video)"), and a version of another length shows its words without timing. Only the song you ask for is looked up, only when you tap.
- **Bookmarks**: mark a spot in an audiobook or a podcast, with a note if you like, and jump back to it later. Bookmarks are included in your backups.
- **Continue listening** on Home: the books and podcasts you've started, newest first, with how much is left. Tap one to pick up where you stopped.

### Your music, your phone
- **Add music from a computer**: Settings → *Add music from a computer*. Open the address shown on the screen in any browser on the same Wi-Fi and drop your files: they're saved in Music/audifac and appear in your library at once. No cable, no app to install. It only works while that screen is open.
- **Share** a song file with any app, or **Delete** it, from the song menu (Android asks you to confirm).
- **Shortcuts**: long-press the audifac icon for *Shuffle all*, *Favorites*, *Soirée* and *Search*.
- **Quick Settings tile**: play or pause audifac from the notification shade, with the song's name.

### Soirée
- **Guest requests**: switch on *Take requests* and show the QR code. Guests on your Wi-Fi scan it, search your library and ask for songs, with their name. You accept or decline each one; accepted songs join the evening where they fit.
- **Keep apart**: pairs of artists that never play close together.

### Looks and comfort
- **Wallpaper colours (Material You)**: Settings → Appearance tints the whole app with your wallpaper's colours (Android 12 and later). Off by default.
- **Landscape and tablets**: in landscape the player puts the artwork beside the controls. On wide screens the tabs move to a side rail, and on tablets the player stays open next to your library.
- **Screen readers**: the player buttons, chips, mini player and Home cards now say what they are. Shuffle and repeat say if they're on, and the seek bar reads the time.

### Faster
- Changing a filter chip, the sort order, the theme or the crossfade no longer rebuilds your whole library behind the scenes, so big libraries respond faster.
- Building the artist list and sorting songs take much less work, and crossfades create less memory churn. What you see and hear is exactly the same.

### Fixes
- Progress bars at 0 % looked full; they now look empty.
