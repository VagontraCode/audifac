## What's new in audifac 0.5.0: the Soirée update

### Plan a soirée
- **A mix from your songs that ends on the dance floor** (Library → Playlists → "Plan a soirée"): choose how long the evening lasts and its shape (*House party*, *Dinner → dance*, *Straight to the floor*, *With a cool-down*, *Wind down*), and audifac picks songs from your phone that fit each moment, in an order that flows.
- **Draw the evening**: drag the points of the curve to make it calmer or wilder at any moment; the list follows.
- **Make it yours**: swap a song for one of five that fit the same moment, keep a song where it is, remove, reorder or add songs, ask for another mix, and save it as a normal playlist.
- **Songs from** the whole library or chosen playlists. Voice notes, recordings, talks and tracks without a beat stay out.
- **Play it**: songs play in the planned order, and audifac offers a 6 s crossfade if it's off so each song blends into the next.

### Read the room
- While a soirée plays, tap the Soirée chip in the player: **More energy**, **Keep it here** or **Calm it down** reshape the rest of the evening. The current song always plays to its end.
- **Learn from soirées** (Settings): songs skipped during a soirée count as less danceable next time, songs played through at the peak as more. You can switch it off.

### Tempo, energy and danceability
- **Analyse songs for soirées** (Settings → Library): the tempo, groove and energy of every song are measured on the phone, in the background, from three short excerpts. Nothing is uploaded and your music files are never modified.
- The plan **fills in and keeps improving** while the analysis runs (locked songs stay where you put them).
- **Song info** shows tempo, energy and danceability. Fix a wrong tempo with **Tap tempo** (×½ / ×2 for a tempo read at half or double speed), or nudge a song **more or less danceable**; "Back to measured" undoes it.
- **Smart playlists** can filter on tempo, energy and danceability.

Tuned for Afrobeats / Amapiano and Coupé-décalé / Ndombolo / Makossa. Scores come from the sound, not from knowing the song: a slow song everyone sings along to scores low until you nudge it or a soirée teaches it.
