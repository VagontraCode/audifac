## What's new in audifac 0.6.0

### Whole albums in the queue
- **Play next / Add to queue / Add to playlist** for a whole album, artist, folder or playlist: tap ⋮ on its page, or long-press an album or artist in the Library.
- **Queue → "+ Add an album"**: add an album to what's playing without stopping the music.
- **During a soirée**, what you add joins the evening: *Add to queue* spreads the songs where they fit the mood, *Play next* plays them right after the current song. "More energy" and "Calm it down" keep them.

### Soirée settings
- **Leave out religious music**: one switch on the Soirée screen. Gospel, worship and louange are found from the genre tag and words like Jésus, Seigneur, Nzambe or Alléluia, and a gospel artist's other songs come along. Tap **Check** to see every song left out and keep any of them in. Something missed? Choose *Religious: leave out* on that song.
- **Songs from folders**: build a soirée from any mix of Favourites, playlists and folders (with their subfolders), or from a folder's or a playlist's menu with **Plan a soirée with these songs**. Their songs are analysed first.
- **Add a song** to a soirée even before it has been analysed.
