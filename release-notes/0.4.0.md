## What's new in audifac 0.4.0: the audiophile update

### Sound
- **High-quality resampling** (Settings → Sound): songs are converted to your phone's output rate by audifac's own linear-phase resampler instead of Android's.
- **Dither** when the output is reduced to 16-bit, so quiet passages stay clean.
- **Tone controls**: bass, treble, tilt, and loudness compensation that keeps low volume full-bodied.
- **Stereo tools**: width, channel delay and polarity per side.
- **Convolution**: load an impulse response (WAV) per output device, for room or headphone correction.
- **Linear-phase EQ** (Equalizer → Gain), with no phase shift between bands.
- **Compressor** for noisy places and night listening.
- **Find my headphones**: search the AutoEQ database (about 9,000 models) and apply a correction in one tap.
- **Surround files** are folded down to stereo before any processing.
- **Remember per album or artist**: a volume-matching mode (e.g. album gain for a live album) or a playback speed (e.g. 1.4× for an audiobook).

### See what you hear
- **Signal path** shows levels after the EQ and the exact format sent to Android.
- **Spectrogram and stereo scope**: the Signal sheet adds a scrolling spectrogram, a goniometer and a stereo correlation meter.
- **Waveform seek bar**: the player's seek bar shows the song's loudness over time.
- **Dynamic range (DR)** for every song, and **fake-lossless detection**: lossless files that were made from an MP3 or AAC are flagged.
- **Artwork viewer**: tap the cover for the full-resolution picture, with its size and format, and pinch to zoom.

### Library
- **Sort by resolution, bitrate or dynamic range**, and a "Fake lossless?" filter.
- **Duplicates** (Settings → Library): songs you have in several files, with the best copy suggested. Nothing is deleted.
- **Edit tags** (song menu): fix titles, artists, albums, genre, year and track. Changes are kept in audifac and in your backups; **your music files are never modified**.
- **CUE sheets**: one-file albums are split into their tracks and play one after another. On Android 11+, add the album's folder in Settings → Folders so audifac can read the `.cue`.
- **M3U / M3U8 playlists**: import them from another app or a computer, and export any playlist.
- **More tags read**: `.mka` / `.webm`, `.opus` / `.ogg`, and DSD `.dff` titles.

### Fixes
- No more freezes, skipped songs or stutters with the equalizer and crossfade on; effects now apply at once.
- No more short stutters in the middle of some MP3s.
